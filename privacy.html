Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. Overview

This application is a privately managed, self-hosted instance of Actual Budget, used exclusively for personal financial management.

The application is intended solely for my own use and is not offered as a public or commercial service.

2. Financial Information

The application may process personal financial information including:

  • Bank account information
  • Account balances
  • Transaction histories
  • Income and expenditure
  • Personal budgets and savings information

This information is used exclusively for personal budgeting, financial monitoring and money management.

3. Bank Account Connections

Bank account connections are facilitated through Enable Banking.

Access to banking information requires my authorisation through the relevant financial institution.

Enable Banking processes information necessary to provide bank connectivity and transaction synchronisation.

Banking information is accessed only for my personal financial management purposes.

4. Data Storage

Actual Budget is hosted on my own privately managed server.

Budgeting information is stored within my self-hosted environment rather than being intentionally published to a publicly accessible website.

Financial records are not sold, distributed for advertising purposes, or intentionally disclosed to unauthorised third parties.

Third-party infrastructure and banking connectivity providers may process information necessary to deliver their respective services.

5. Security and Access

The application is intended exclusively for my personal use.

Access to Actual Budget is protected by authentication credentials.

Remote access is provided through Tailscale, a private encrypted networking service.

Tailscale access is restricted to authorised devices and users within my private network.

The application is not intentionally exposed to the public internet.

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect personal financial information against unauthorised access.

Although these safeguards are designed to restrict access, no computer system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Public Access

This privacy policy is publicly available for transparency and to support the Enable Banking application registration.

Publication of this policy does not provide public access to Actual Budget or to the financial information stored within the application.

Personal bank details, transaction records and account balances are not published as part of this policy.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Financial information is retained for as long as it is required for my personal budgeting purposes.

As the owner and sole intended user of this application, I control the retention and deletion of information stored within my Actual Budget instance.

Information processed by external banking connectivity providers may be subject to their own retention policies.

8. Third-Party Services

The application uses third-party services where necessary for secure connectivity and banking integration.

These may include:

  • Actual Budget - Personal budgeting software
  • Enable Banking - Banking connectivity
  • Tailscale - Private network access
  • GitHub Pages - Hosting this privacy policy

Each provider operates under its own applicable privacy and security policies.

9. Commercial Use

This application is not operated as a commercial financial service.

It is used solely for managing my personal finances.

No financial information is collected from members of the public, and no financial information is sold.

10. Contact

This application is privately operated.

For questions relating to this privacy policy, contact the application owner using the contact information registered with Enable Banking.

11. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the application's functionality, security arrangements or data processing practices change.

The latest version will be published at this URL.
